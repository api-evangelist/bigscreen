Happy holidays from the Bigscreen team! As a special end-of-year release, we’re excited to announce that Dynamic Foveated Rendering (DFR) is now available in early access for Bigscreen Beyond 2e . Bigscreen Beyond 2e has built-in eyetracking which has two main use cases: social VR (in games like VRChat) and performance improvements (for simulation games like iRacing and DCS World).
