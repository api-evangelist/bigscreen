As mentioned in last week's production update , after months of challenges with engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, and tariffs, we're thrilled to announce the Bigscreen Beyond 2 with Universal-Fit Cushions are now shipping! As a contextual reminder, the Bigscreen Beyond 2 can be purchased with one of two options: with the Custom-Fit Cushion, or with the Halo Mount and Universal-Fit Cushion. We started shipping Beyond 2 with the Custom-Fit Cushion 5 months ago and today, we've started shipping Beyond 2 orders with the Universal-Fit Cushion.
